Common Whales in the Gulf of Maine

  • Humpback Whale
  • Fin Whale
  • Minke Whale

Humpback Whale

The Humpback Whale is a large whale that is common in the Gulf of Maine. They primarily feed on krill, small fish and plankton.

Fin Whale

The Fin Whale is another large whale that is common in the Gulf of Maine. They primarily feed on krill, small fish and squid.

Minke Whale

The Minke Whale is a smaller whale that is also common in the Gulf of Maine. Minke Whales are also known to eat krill, small fish and squid.
